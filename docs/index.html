🛡️ Melbourne Property Buyer Due Diligence Guide

Essential guidance for navigating underquoting traps, building defects, and Victorian contracts.

⚠️

1. How Victorian Underquoting Works

Under the Estate Agents Act 1980, an agent's Statement of Information (SOI) must list an indicative selling price and 3 recent comparable sales. However, agents often deliberately quote the bottom 10% of their vendor's reserve to manufacture auction competition. If the AVM Fair Value sits >11% above the top guide, the listing is almost certainly auction bait.

🚪

2. Inboard / Windowless 2nd Bedrooms

Prior to Victoria's 2017 Better Apartments Design Standards (BADS), developers built apartments where the 2nd bedroom had no external window, using "borrowed light" through frosted sliding glass or internal corridors. These suffer poor ventilation, lower resale liquidity, and bank valuation discounts.

🏢

3. Combustible Cladding & Owners Corp Levies

High-rise precincts in Docklands, Southbank, and Melbourne CBD frequently carry special levies for flammable aluminum composite cladding rectification. Always check Section 32 Owners Corporation certificates for special levies, sinking fund balances, and annual fees exceeding $6,000/year.

📏

4. The 50 sqm Bank Lending Threshold

Most major Australian banks require an internal floor area of at least 50 square meters (excluding balconies and car spaces) to approve a standard 5% to 10% loan-to-value ratio (LVR). Properties under 50 sqm often require a 20%+ deposit and are strictly excluded from the Federal 5% deposit scheme.
