🛡️ Melbourne Property Buyer Due Diligence Guide

Essential guidance for identifying underquoting traps, hidden building defects, and bank mortgage lending limits across Greater Melbourne.

⚠️

1. How Victorian Underquoting Works

Under the Victorian Estate Agents Act 1980, agents must publish a Statement of Information (SOI) with an indicative selling guide and 3 comparable sales. However, agents routinely quote the bottom 10% of the vendor's reserve to artificially drive auction crowds. When the AVM Fair Value sits >11% above the upper guide, treat the property as auction bait and budget for the true market value.

Buyer Action: Never pay for a building inspection or contract review until verifying comparable settled sales within 2 km.
🚪

2. Inboard / Windowless 2nd Bedrooms

Prior to Victoria's 2017 Better Apartments Design Standards (BADS), developers constructed thousands of CBD and Docklands units where second bedrooms lacked external windows, using "borrowed light" via frosted glass dividers. These suffer poor airflow, lower rental appeal, and bank valuation haircuts.

Buyer Action: Check floorplans carefully. Every habitable bedroom should have direct perimeter glazing to the open sky.
🏢

3. Combustible Cladding & Owners Corp Levies

High-rise precincts in Docklands, Southbank, and Melbourne CBD frequently carry special levies for flammable aluminum composite cladding rectification. Always check Section 32 Owners Corporation certificates for pending cladding notices, sinking fund balances, and annual fees exceeding $6,000/year.

Buyer Action: Inspect Section 32 OC certificates for special levies or active Cladding Safety Victoria (CSV) disputes.
📏

4. The 50 sqm Bank Lending Threshold

Most major Australian banks require an internal floor area of at least 50 square meters (excluding balconies and car spaces) to approve a standard 5% to 10% loan-to-value ratio (LVR). Properties under 50 sqm often require a 20%+ deposit and are strictly excluded from the Federal 5% deposit scheme.

Buyer Action: Request architectural strata plans to confirm internal living dimensions before bidding on studio or compact 1-beds.
